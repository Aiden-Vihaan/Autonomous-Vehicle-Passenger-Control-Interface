## Decision 001 — Research Focus

### Decision

The research component will focus on the effect of explainable AI feedback on passenger interaction with highly automated vehicles.

### Primary Outcomes

The initial outcomes of interest are:

- Comprehension
- Interaction performance
- Trust calibration
- Perceived workload
- Perceived transparency

### Reason

The original project already focuses on autonomous vehicle passenger control, trust, transparency, unexpected events, and explainability. The research direction extends these existing themes into an empirically evaluable Human Factors question without changing the identity or core scope of the internship project.

### Status

Accepted

## Decision 002 — Explanation Conditions

### Decision

The initial experimental concept will compare three levels of explanation:

1. No explanation
2. Basic explanation
3. Contextual explanation

### Reason

A three-condition comparison provides a structured way to investigate whether increasing explanation detail produces differences in passenger understanding, trust, workload, and interaction performance.

### Status

Initial — subject to literature review and pilot evaluation.

---

## Decision 003 — Research Integrity

### Decision

The project will not make claims about production autonomous vehicle safety, regulatory compliance, certification, or real-world vehicle performance unless independently verified and directly supported.

### Reason

The project is a UX/Human Factors research and design project rather than a vehicle engineering validation study.

### Status

Accepted
