Research Constructs

Purpose

This document defines the major constructs used in the project.

---

Explainability

Definition:

The degree to which passengers understand why the autonomous vehicle behaves in a particular way.

Possible indicators:

- Perceived understanding
- Explanation usefulness
- Transparency rating

---

Situation Awareness

Definition:

Passenger understanding of:

- Current state
- System intentions
- Future behaviour

Reference:

Endsley (1995)

---

Trust Calibration

Definition:

Alignment between:

- Passenger trust
- Actual system capability

Trust should be appropriate rather than maximized.

---

Cognitive Workload

Definition:

Mental effort required to understand and interact with the system.

Measurement:

- NASA-TLX

---

Transparency

Definition:

How clearly the system communicates:

- State
- Intentions
- Uncertainty

---

Comprehension

Definition:

Passenger understanding of:

- System behaviour
- Reasoning
- Future actions

---

Interaction Performance

Possible measures:

- Response time
- Task completion
- Error rate
- Accuracy

---

Accessibility

Definition:

Ability of different users to understand and use the interface.

Includes:

- Visual accessibility
- Auditory accessibility
- Cognitive accessibility
