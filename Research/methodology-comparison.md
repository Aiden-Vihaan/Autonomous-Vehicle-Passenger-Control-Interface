Methodology Comparison

Purpose

This document compares the methodologies used in relevant autonomous-vehicle Human Factors research.

The purpose is to identify methodological patterns that can inform the present study.

---

1. Simulator Experiments

Simulator experiments appear frequently in AV Human Factors research.

Advantages:

- Controlled environment
- Repeatable scenarios
- Precise experimental manipulation
- Lower physical risk
- Easier comparison between conditions

Disadvantages:

- Lower ecological validity than real-world driving
- Participants may behave differently from passengers in an actual robotaxi
- Simulator sickness can influence results

---

2. Real-Vehicle Studies

Strauch et al. included real-traffic passenger research as well as simulator research.

Advantages:

- Higher ecological validity
- Real passenger behaviour
- Real environmental context

Disadvantages:

- More difficult to control variables
- Higher logistical complexity
- Greater safety requirements
- More difficult to reproduce identical scenarios

---

3. Within-Subject Designs

Du et al. used a within-subject experimental design.

Advantages:

- Each participant acts as their own comparison
- Individual differences are reduced
- Often useful when sample sizes are constrained

Disadvantages:

- Carryover effects
- Learning effects
- Participants may infer the purpose of the experiment

---

4. Between-Subject Designs

Advantages:

- Reduced learning/carryover effects
- Each participant experiences fewer experimental conditions

Disadvantages:

- Greater individual variability
- Usually requires more participants to obtain comparable statistical precision

---

5. Survey / Structural Modelling

Lee et al. used a larger participant sample and structural equation modelling.

This approach is useful for studying:

- Perceived explainability
- Trust
- Acceptance
- Perceived intelligence
- Perceived usefulness

However, survey-based perception research does not directly replace controlled behavioural experimentation.

---

Methodological Pattern

Across the literature:

Controlled Experiment
        +
Specific Manipulation
        +
Validated/Structured Measures
        ↓
Evidence about Human Response

This supports our decision to build an experimental prototype rather than relying only on usability feedback.

---

Preliminary Choice for Our Study

The current project should prioritize:

Controlled prototype-based experiment

rather than:

- Open-ended usability review
- Pure design critique
- Uncontrolled questionnaire

The exact experimental design will be finalized after:

1. Selecting final variables.
2. Selecting measurement instruments.
3. Determining feasible sample size.
4. Designing scenarios.
5. Conducting a pilot.

---

Methodological Principle

We should not select the method because it looks impressive.

We should select the simplest method capable of answering the research question reliably.
