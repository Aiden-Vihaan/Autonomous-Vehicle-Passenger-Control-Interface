Day 9 — Methodological Literature Analysis

Objective

Move from literature synthesis to methodological extraction.

The focus was on understanding how previous researchers investigated explanation, trust, passenger behaviour, workload, and attention in autonomous-vehicle contexts.

---

Papers Analysed

1. Du et al. (2019)

Primary contribution:

Explanation timing.

Method:

Within-subject driving-simulator experiment with 32 participants.

Project relevance:

Supports experimental manipulation of pre-action versus post-action explanations.

---

2. Ha et al. (2020)

Primary contribution:

Explanation type × perceived risk.

Method:

Simulator experiment using three explanation types and four risk contexts.

Project relevance:

Demonstrates that explanation effects can depend on situational risk.

---

3. Strauch et al. (2019)

Primary contribution:

Passenger gaze and trust.

Method:

Field and simulator investigations.

Project relevance:

Provides direct passenger-focused evidence and cautions against treating gaze as a direct substitute for trust.

---

4. Hong et al. (2025)

Primary contribution:

Multimodal explanation in fully autonomous passenger contexts.

Project relevance:

Shows that modality effectiveness can depend on passenger engagement in non-driving activities.

---

5. Lee et al. (2024)

Primary contribution:

Perceived explainability and AV trust/acceptance.

Method:

Survey with 399 participants and structural equation modelling.

Project relevance:

Supports explainability as an important construct but does not directly establish real-time interface effects.

---

Major Methodological Learning

Previous studies differ substantially in:

- Participant role
- Automation level
- Experimental environment
- Explanation design
- Measurement
- Scenario risk
- Interaction task

Therefore, findings should not be combined without considering context.

---

Major Design Decision

The project will prioritize a controlled experimental design in which:

- The scenario is controlled.
- The autonomous event is controlled.
- The explanation is manipulated.
- Human outcomes are measured.

---

Important Limitation

The current literature review is based on accessible publication records, abstracts, and available academic metadata for some papers.

Full-text methodological details will only be added when the actual paper is accessible and reviewed.

No participant count, statistical result, questionnaire, or methodological detail will be invented from an abstract.
