Overview

This project investigated passenger-facing explanations for unexpected autonomous-vehicle behaviour from a Human Factors perspective.

The intervention isolates explanation presentation while holding the underlying vehicle behaviour constant.

---

Explanation as Human-Automation Interaction

Traditional interfaces primarily communicate actions available to the user.

Autonomous systems introduce another relationship:

System
 ↓
Decision
 ↓
Passenger interpretation

The explanation interface therefore becomes part of the human-automation relationship.

---

Information Density

More information does not necessarily create better understanding.

An explanation has two competing functions:

1. reduce uncertainty,
2. introduce information-processing demand.

The design objective is therefore appropriate information density.

---

Objective and Perceived Understanding

Objective comprehension and perceived understanding must remain separate.

A passenger may feel informed while misunderstanding the system.

A passenger may also correctly understand an event while reporting lower confidence.

---

Trust

Trust is interpreted as calibration.

Appropriate confidence
+
Accurate understanding
+
Awareness of limitations
=
Calibrated trust

---

Workload

A successful explanation reduces uncertainty without creating excessive cognitive demand.
