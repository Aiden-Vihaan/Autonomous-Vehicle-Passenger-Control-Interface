# Theoretical Framework

## Project

**Autonomous Vehicle Passenger Control Interface**

## Purpose

This document defines the theoretical areas that may be used to analyse passenger interaction with highly automated vehicles.

The final theoretical framework will be selected after reviewing relevant academic literature.

Theories will only be retained when they directly contribute to understanding the research questions or making defensible design decisions.

---

# Candidate Theoretical Areas

## 1. Trust in Automation

Trust in automation provides a framework for understanding how people form expectations about automated systems and decide whether to rely on them.

### Relevance

This area is directly relevant to the research question concerning how explanations may influence passenger trust and trust calibration.

### Project Connection

The project will investigate whether communicating autonomous-system behaviour can help passengers develop an appropriate understanding of system capabilities and limitations.

---

## 2. Situation Awareness

Situation awareness provides a framework for understanding how people perceive relevant information, comprehend its meaning, and anticipate future states.

### Relevance

Passengers may need to understand:

- What is happening
- What the vehicle is doing
- Why the vehicle is doing it
- What may happen next
- Whether passenger action is required

### Project Connection

The interface will explore how information about vehicle intent and unexpected events can support passenger understanding.

---

## 3. Cognitive Workload

Cognitive workload concerns the mental demands associated with performing tasks and processing information.

### Relevance

Explanations may improve understanding while simultaneously increasing information-processing demands.

### Project Connection

The evaluation will therefore consider whether additional explanation provides useful information without creating unnecessary workload.

---

## 4. Human-Automation Interaction

Human-automation interaction provides a broader framework for understanding the relationship between human users and automated systems.

### Relevance

The passenger is not simply a conventional driver or passive observer. The interaction role changes as vehicle automation increases.

### Project Connection

The project examines how the interface can support an appropriate passenger role within a highly automated system.

---

## 5. Explainable AI

Explainable AI provides concepts for communicating information about AI-system behaviour in ways that may support human understanding and interaction.

### Relevance

Explainability is the central independent factor being explored in this project.

### Project Connection

The project compares different levels of explanation and examines their potential effects on comprehension, trust, workload, and interaction performance.

---

# Theoretical Relationship

The current conceptual model is:

**Autonomous System Behaviour**

↓

**System Explanation**

↓

**Passenger Perception**

↓

**Understanding / Situation Awareness**

↓

**Trust and Workload**

↓

**Interaction Behaviour**

This model is preliminary and will be refined based on the literature review.

---

# Theory Selection Criteria

A theoretical framework will be included in the final project only if it:

1. Has established relevance to the research problem.
2. Is supported by credible academic literature.
3. Provides explanatory value for the research questions.
4. Can inform either research methodology or interface design.
5. Does not exist merely to increase the number of theories cited.
