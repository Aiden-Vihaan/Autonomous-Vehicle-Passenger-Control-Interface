# Research Hypotheses

## Project

**Autonomous Vehicle Passenger Control Interface**

## Purpose

This document defines the initial hypotheses for the experimental component of the project.

The hypotheses are derived from the research questions and will be evaluated through the planned prototype and user study.

---

## H1 — Comprehension

**Contextual AI explanations will result in higher passenger comprehension of autonomous vehicle behaviour than minimal or no explanations.**

### Rationale

Passengers may have difficulty interpreting autonomous vehicle behaviour when the system communicates only its action without sufficient context.

Providing information about both the relevant event and the vehicle's resulting action may help passengers construct a clearer understanding of system behaviour.

---

## H2 — Interaction Performance

**Contextual AI explanations will improve passenger interaction performance during unexpected events compared with minimal or no explanation.**

Interaction performance may be assessed through measures such as:

- Task completion
- Response accuracy
- Response time
- Interaction errors

The direction and magnitude of any observed effect will be determined empirically.

---

## H3 — Trust Calibration

**Providing relevant contextual explanations will influence passenger trust in the autonomous system by improving perceived transparency and understanding.**

The expected outcome is not simply higher trust.

The research will investigate whether explanations support **appropriate or calibrated trust**, where passenger confidence is consistent with the information communicated by the system.

---

## H4 — Cognitive Workload

**Additional explanation content may influence perceived cognitive workload during unexpected events.**

The research will examine whether contextual explanations provide useful information without creating excessive information-processing demands.

This hypothesis is therefore evaluated as a potential trade-off between:

**More information → Better understanding**

and

**More information → Higher workload**

---

## H5 — Explanation Level

**Different levels of explanation will produce different outcomes across comprehension, trust, workload, and interaction performance.**

The initial comparison consists of:

1. No explanation
2. Basic explanation
3. Contextual explanation

The study will determine whether increasing explanation detail produces measurable benefits or costs.

---

# Hypothesis Summary

| Hypothesis | Independent Factor | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Explanation level | Comprehension |
| H2 | Explanation level | Interaction performance |
| H3 | Explanation level | Trust / transparency |
| H4 | Explanation level | Perceived workload |
| H5 | Explanation level | Overall trade-off across outcomes |

---

# Important Note

These are **pre-study hypotheses**.

They are not conclusions.

The final analysis will report whether the collected evidence supports, partially supports, or does not support each hypothesis.

The hypotheses may be refined if the literature review or pilot study identifies methodological problems with the initial formulation. Any changes will be documented rather than silently replacing the original hypotheses.
