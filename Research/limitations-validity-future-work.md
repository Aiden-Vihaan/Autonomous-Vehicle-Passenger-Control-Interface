Limitations, Validity, and Future Work

Internal Validity

Potential threats include order effects, learning effects, technical failures, and protocol deviations.

Mitigation includes counterbalancing/randomisation, controlled scenarios, documented exclusions, and version freezing.

---

Construct Validity

Trust and workload are multidimensional constructs.

Self-report measures therefore complement rather than replace behavioural/comprehension measures.

---

Ecological Validity

A prototype cannot reproduce every property of an actual moving autonomous vehicle.

Real-world factors include:

- acceleration,
- vibration,
- ambient sound,
- passenger activity,
- social context,
- environmental uncertainty.

---

External Validity

A limited participant sample cannot establish universal passenger behaviour.

Future work should broaden:

- age,
- technology familiarity,
- accessibility needs,
- cultural/language backgrounds,
- autonomous-vehicle experience.

---

Scenario Coverage

Future scenarios should include:

- emergency vehicles,
- pedestrians,
- cyclists,
- construction,
- unexpected obstacles,
- rerouting,
- degraded perception,
- weather,
- connectivity loss.

---

Accessibility

The design includes accessibility requirements, but strong accessibility claims require testing with actual assistive-technology users.

---

Autonomous Driving Scope

This project evaluates the passenger interface.

It does not establish:

- autonomous-driving safety,
- perception accuracy,
- planning accuracy,
- vehicle certification,
- regulatory compliance.

---

Threats-to-Validity Matrix

Validity| Threat| Mitigation
Internal| Order effects| Counterbalancing
Construct| Self-report limitation| Multiple measures
Ecological| Prototype environment| Future vehicle study
External| Sample diversity| Broader recruitment
Statistical| Sample size| Effect sizes + uncertainty
Accessibility| Limited assistive-tech testing| Dedicated validation
Technical| Interface latency| Timestamp instrumentation
