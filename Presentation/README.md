Final Presentation

Slide 1 — Title

Designing Passenger-Facing Explanations for Autonomous Vehicle Behaviour

Human Factors approach to understanding, trust, and cognitive workload.

---

Slide 2 — The Problem

Autonomous vehicles can perform behaviours that passengers may perceive as unexpected.

The interface therefore needs to bridge:

Machine behaviour
        ↓
Passenger interpretation

---

Slide 3 — Design Challenge

How can the interface explain unexpected behaviour without:

- overwhelming the passenger?
- creating unnecessary alarm?
- exposing irrelevant technical information?
- reducing accessibility?
- encouraging inappropriate trust?

---

Slide 4 — Research Question

«How does the presentation of explanations for unexpected autonomous-vehicle behaviour affect passenger understanding, trust, and cognitive workload?»

Slide 18 — Final Takeaway

The goal is not to make the vehicle explain everything.

The goal is to provide the right explanation, at the right moment, in a form that supports passenger understanding without unnecessary cognitive demand.
