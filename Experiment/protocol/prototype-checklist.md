---

Research Integrity

The project must not:

- fabricate participant data;
- fabricate statistical results;
- invent participant counts;
- selectively remove inconvenient observations;
- change hypotheses after observing results without documentation;
- claim real-world autonomous-driving safety validation;
- claim certification or regulatory compliance that was not established.
