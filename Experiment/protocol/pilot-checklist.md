7. Observation Requirements

During the pilot, the experimenter will record:

- unexpected participant confusion;
- navigation problems;
- unclear terminology;
- difficulty interpreting autonomous behaviour;
- difficulty understanding explanations;
- measurement interruptions;
- accessibility barriers;
- technical failures;
- timing problems;
- experimenter intervention;
- deviations from the standardized protocol.

Observations will be recorded as observations, not converted into unsupported quantitative findings.

---

- [x] Critical interaction path is defined

- [x] Pilot observation process is defined
- [x] Revision decision process is defined
- [x] Research-integrity boundary is defined

---

9. Final Pilot Gate

The project may proceed from pilot preparation to pilot execution only after confirming that:

«The prototype can present the experimental conditions consistently, participants can complete the defined task and measures, and potential technical or procedural problems can be documented without confusing pilot observations with formal experimental evidence.»
